Map

The mosque map is drawn by MapLibre GL JS, with map data and tiles from
OpenFreeMap, OpenMapTiles and OpenStreetMap contributors. Arabic labels are
shaped by the Mapbox RTL text plugin. Both libraries are bundled in the app.
